What We Do

Truck & Fleet Detailing Services

From a single prime mover to a yard full of utes, done on site and done properly.

🚛
Wash, Degrease, Cut & Polish

Exterior wash and degrease, then a machine cut and polish to bring oxidised, chalky paint and fibreglass back to gloss. Cabs, prime movers, rigids and trailers. Red dirt removal is available as an add-on.

✨
Alloy Polishing

Bullbars, fuel tanks, rims and other aluminium taken back to a mirror finish, worked through progressive grits and polished by hand.

🧽
Cab Interior Detail

Seats, dash, floors and door trims properly cleaned out, so drivers climb into a cab that's clean.

🛻
Fleet Utes & 4WDs

Got work utes and 4WDs in the same yard? We do them on the same visit, while we're already set up on site.
